Let it "cook"

Let the wetted mix "cook" for 1-2 weeks before transplanting. Microbes (Dr. Earth includes beneficial microbes and mycorrhizae) break amendments into plant-available nutrients, and early nitrogen, heat, pH and moisture settle so seedling roots aren't burned. Keep it moist (not soggy), covered and warm; a little earthy smell is normal.

More bags?

Multiply every amount by the number of bags.

Within label rates

Watch For

Burnt or crispy tips

Too strong. Back off feeding, water plain.

Pale or yellow leaves early

Check watering and pH before adding more.

Lower leaves yellow late in flower

Normal fade. Leave it.
